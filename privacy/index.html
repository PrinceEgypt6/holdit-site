Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Hold It is made by Dialed Development PTY LTD (“we”). This policy explains what the Hold It iPhone app does with your information. The short version: we don’t collect any data.

Everything stays on your phone

Hold It has no account, no sign-in, no server and no analytics. It contains no advertising and no third-party tracking code. We never receive your photos, videos, sound, camera feed or anything you do in the app.

Camera

Hold It uses your camera only while the camera screen is open, to coach the person taking your photo. The live picture is analysed on your iPhone with Apple’s built-in Vision features (body pose, face position, light and level). That analysis is never stored or sent anywhere. Pictures and videos are only recorded when you press the shutter or record button.

Microphone

Hold It uses your microphone only in Video mode, so your videos have sound. The sound stays in that video on your iPhone. We never receive it. If you say no, you can still take photos and record silent videos.

Photos

Hold It asks for “add only” access to your photo library so it can save the photos and videos you take. It can’t see or read the photos already in your library. They are saved only when you take them with Auto-save on, or when you tap Save. When you share one, it goes only where you choose in the iPhone share sheet.

Your answers and settings

Your onboarding answers (for example, who usually takes your photos) and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, so the app can tailor its words and remember your choices. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and handled by Apple through the App Store. We don’t receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy covers purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Hold It is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
