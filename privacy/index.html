Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Hold It is made by Dialed Development PTY LTD (“we”). This policy explains what the Hold It iPhone app does with your information. The short version: your photos, videos and camera feed stay on your iPhone. An account is optional. If you create one, we keep only what’s needed to sign you in.

What stays on your phone

We never receive your photos, videos, sound, camera feed or what you do in the app, whether or not you have an account. Hold It has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code.

Optional accounts

You can use all of Hold It without an account. If you sign in, we collect:

  • Your email address, from Apple, Google or what you type. If you use Apple’s Hide My Email, we get that private address instead.
  • Your name, if Apple or Google shares it when you sign in.
  • A user ID, created for your account, and which sign-in method you used.

We use these only to sign you in and show your account in Settings. They’re linked to your account. We don’t use them for advertising or tracking, and we don’t sell them. A copy of your account details is also kept on your iPhone so Settings can show them straight away.

Who handles account data

  • Google Firebase runs sign-in and stores your account. Firebase may process this data in the United States and other countries: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in email if you choose email. It receives your email address and the message: resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Apple or Google confirm who you are if you sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.

To stop sign-in emails being abused, the sign-in service briefly keeps scrambled (hashed) versions of the email address and internet address asking for a link, with a count of requests. These records are removed automatically, normally within a few days.

Deleting your account

In Hold It, open Settings › Account › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account from Firebase, and for Sign in with Apple it also asks Apple to revoke Hold It’s access. You may be asked to confirm it’s you first. Your photos stay on your iPhone, and your subscription isn’t changed, so cancel it separately if you want to stop paying. Signing out doesn’t delete anything. If you can’t get into the app, email us and we’ll delete your account.

Camera

Hold It uses your camera while the app is open on your screen, to coach the person taking your photo. The live picture is analysed on your iPhone with Apple’s built-in Vision features (body pose, face position, light and level). That analysis is never stored or sent anywhere. Photos and videos are taken when you press the shutter or record button, or automatically if you turn on “Take the photo for me” (Hold It Pro). Live Photos include a moment from just before and after the shot.

Microphone

Hold It uses your microphone only in Video mode, so your videos have sound. The sound stays in that video on your iPhone. We never receive it. If you say no, you can still take photos and record silent videos.

Photos

Hold It asks for “add only” access to your photo library so it can save the photos and videos you take. It can’t see or read the photos already in your library. They are saved only when you take them with Auto-save on, or when you tap Save. When you share one, it goes only where you choose in the iPhone share sheet.

Your Hold It library

The photos and videos you take are also kept in a private folder inside the app, so you can view, enhance and share them. They stay on your iPhone, aren’t included in iCloud backups, and are removed when you delete them in Hold It or delete the app.

AI Enhance

AI Enhance runs entirely on your iPhone, using Apple’s built-in image and Vision tools. Your photos are never uploaded to enhance them, and your original is never changed.

Your answers and settings

Your onboarding answers (for example, who usually takes your photos) and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, so the app can tailor its words and remember your choices. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple doesn’t give us your name, email address or payment details for a purchase, and your subscription isn’t tied to a Hold It account. Apple’s privacy policy covers purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Hold It works without an account, and nobody needs to give us personal information to use it. Accounts aren’t meant for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created one, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
