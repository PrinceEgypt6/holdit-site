How does Hold It work?

Open Hold It, pick a mode (Outfit, Portrait, Close-up or Travel) and hand your phone to whoever is taking the photo. The card at the top tells them one thing at a time: lower, step back, move left, hold still. The ring around the shutter fills as the shot gets better. When it’s right, Hold It says “Hold it… now!” and, with Hold It Pro, takes the photo by itself.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the gear on the camera screen, then Restore purchases. You can also tap “Restore purchases” at the bottom of the Hold It Pro screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Hold It, then tap Cancel Subscription. In Hold It you can also go to Settings › Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged again.

The camera is black, or Hold It says it needs the camera

Hold It needs camera access to guide your photos. Open your iPhone’s Settings › Hold It and turn Camera on. If another app, like a video call, is using the camera, Hold It waits and comes back on its own.

My photos aren’t saving

Open your iPhone’s Settings › Hold It › Photos and choose “Add Photos Only” or “Full Access”. Hold It only ever adds photos; it can’t see your library.

Is anything uploaded?

Your photos, videos and camera feed are never uploaded. An account is optional. If you create one, we keep only your sign-in details, such as your email address.

How do I delete my account?

Open Hold It, tap the gear, then Account › Delete account. Your photos stay on your iPhone. A subscription keeps running until you cancel it. If you can’t sign in, email us and we’ll delete it.
