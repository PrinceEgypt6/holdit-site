My Good Side

Hand them your phone. My Good Side tells them exactly what to do.

My Good Side is the iPhone camera that guides whoever is holding your phone. Hand it to your boyfriend, your best friend, your mum or a stranger, and My Good Side tells them exactly what to do, live: lower, tilt up, step back, move left, find the light, hold still. When everything lines up, the outline turns green and the shot is ready.

No more cut-off feet. No more chins from below. No more blurry, tilted photos.

Live guidance in plain words · a score that climbs as they get it right · Outfit, Portrait, Close-up and Travel modes · a Before/After challenge. Saving, sharing and some features need My Good Side Pro.

Your photos stay on your phone. No uploads, and an account is optional.
