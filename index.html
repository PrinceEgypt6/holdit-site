Hold It

Hand them your phone. Hold It tells them exactly what to do.

Hold It is the iPhone camera that guides whoever is holding your phone. Hand it to your boyfriend, your best friend, your mum or a stranger, and Hold It tells them exactly what to do, live: lower, tilt up, step back, move left, find the light, hold still. When everything lines up it says “Hold it… now!” and takes the shot.

No more cut-off feet. No more chins from below. No more blurry, tilted photos.

Live guidance in plain words · a score that climbs as they get it right · auto-shutter at the perfect moment · Outfit, Portrait, Close-up and Travel modes · a Before/After challenge to share.

Your photos stay on your phone. No uploads, and an account is optional.
